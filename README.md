# MindEase V2 🧠

> A privacy-focused mental wellness platform designed to help students understand their mood, manage stress, track productivity, and maintain healthy daily habits.

## 📌 Overview

MindEase is a student-focused mental wellness application that combines journaling, AI-powered mood analysis, stress monitoring, and productivity tracking in one platform.

The application allows students to record their thoughts through journals, receive AI-based emotional insights, track their mood over time, manage tasks, and optionally share their stress level with an administrator for early support.

A key focus of MindEase is **privacy and user consent**. Students remain in control of whether their stress-level information can be shared with an administrator. Private journal content and personal AI analysis are not exposed to administrators.

## 🔐 Privacy & Consent

Privacy is one of the core principles of MindEase.

Students have control over whether their stress-level information can be shared with administrators.

When sharing is disabled:

- Administrators cannot access the student's stress-level information.
- Journal entries remain private.
- Personal AI analysis remains private.

When sharing is enabled:

- The student's permitted stress-level information can be used by the administrator for wellness monitoring.
- The administrator does not receive the student's private journal content.

This creates a **consent-aware wellness monitoring system** rather than unrestricted access to personal mental-health-related information.
